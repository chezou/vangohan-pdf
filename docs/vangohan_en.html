Hainanese Chicken Rice

Things you need to prepare

  • Rice (2 rice cups are recommended)

Instructions

  1. Wash rice, and add water for the amount of rice in your rice cooker, then Mix Seasoning①
  2. Put chicken and green onion on top, and cook rice
  3. When cooked, remove green onion and chicken. Cut chicken in pieces.
  4. Arrange rice on a plate, put chicken, and pour Sauce④ on top with vegetables

Western Style Seasoned Rice

Things you need to prepare

  • Salt
  • 1 cup of rice : 150g/180ml (1.5cups for 3 people)
  • Cheese (Optional)

Instructions

  1. Put washed rice Seasoning① and bacon in a rice cooker pot, add water until the line for 1cup/1.5cup of rice then mix it well.
  2. Put Vegetables② on top then start the rice cooker
  3. Add Seasoning③ when rice is cooked and mix everything gently
  4. Adjust taste with salt and cheese as you prefer

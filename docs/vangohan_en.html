Fried Chicken with Graded Daikon and Lime Sauce

Things you need to prepare

  • Cooking oil
  • Salt & pepper

Instructions

  1. Marinate chicken with Seasoning① for 10 mins, then salt & pepper and coat chicken with potato starch①
  2. Pre-heat about 1-2cm of cooking oil in a pan or a pot at 180℃/350F (dip the handle of a wooden spoon or a chopstick into the oil, and the oil starts steadily bubbling, then the oil is hot enough)
  3. Fry about 3-5 mins each side of chicken. Please make sure the chicken is well cooked
  4. Bring Seasoning④ to a boil in a pan or microwave and toss it with freshly fried chicken in a bowl then arrange on a plate
  5. Pour grated Daikon on top and add vegetables on the side

Pork With Mustard Sauce

Things you need to prepare

  • Water

Instructions

  1. Boil Pork in a pot and drain water.
  2. Add Seasoning ② and carrots & ginger② in a bowl with boiled pork and mix them well
  3. Arrange it on a plate with Cabbage and Japanese basil

Starchy Rice Bowl Sauce

Things you need to prepare

Instructions

Ginger Pork Stir-Fried

Things you need to prepare

  • Cooking oil

Instructions

  1. Stir-fry onion on a pan with cooking oil on medium heat until it’s soft
  2. Coat pork with flour and cook until lightly cooked
  3. Add Seasoning③, then cook on low-medium heat until the sauce is thickened. Arrange it with vegetables on a plate.

Teriyaki Egg Meatballs

Things you need to prepare

  • Cooking oil
  • Salt & pepper

Instructions

  1. Mix meat, onion, Seasoning① and salt & pepper in a bawl until they get sticky, and form into bite-sized meatballs
  2. Cook the meatballs all the way through on medium heat in a pan with cooking oil, and add vegetable and cook well
  3. Wipe extra oil with paper towel then add Seasoning③ and cook it down
  4. Put Seasoning④ on the meatballs and cook in the oven toaster until the Seasoning④ gets slightly browned
